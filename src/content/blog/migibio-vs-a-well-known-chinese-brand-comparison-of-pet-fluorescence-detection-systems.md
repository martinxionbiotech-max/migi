Migibio vs. A Well-Known Chinese Brand

Number of Projects:

Migibio:

48 projects already launched, with over 10 in development.

The Other Brand:

Only 22 projects launched.

Storage Conditions:

Migibio:

All projects can be stored at room temperature.

The Other Brand:

Six projects require refrigeration (SDMA, T4, Canine TSH, COR, Canine Progesterone, Canine D-Dimer).

Sample Preprocessing for SDMA:

Migibio:

No preprocessing required.

The Other Brand:

Preprocessing is necessary—samples must be mixed with a pretreatment solution, then centrifuged for 5 minutes.

Software Adaptation:

Migibio:

Fully compatible with Chinese Sogou input method.

The Other Brand:

Only compatible with English input, making it less convenient for domestic users.

Report Generation and Printing:

Migibio:

Can generate A4-sized reports on a computer, allowing customization of hospital name and logo, as well as print thermal paper receipts.

The Other Brand:

Only supports printing thermal paper receipts.

Software Updates:

Migibio:

Supports online updates.

The Other Brand:

Updates require a local USB drive.

Cloud Chip Technology (Standard Curves):

Migibio:

Features cloud chip technology. If a chip is lost, it can be downloaded online by connecting to Wi-Fi. It also supports custom standard curves based on client-defined reference ranges.

The Other Brand:

Standard curves are printed on the test cards and cannot be customized for clients.

Vaccine Antibody Testing:

Migibio:

Offers both single-panel (three-item antibody) and combined-panel (three-in-one card) tests, making it more convenient for hospitals.

The Other Brand:

Only offers single-panel (three-item antibody) tests.

Equipment Channels:

Migibio:

Provides single-channel and six-channel devices to meet the testing needs of medium to large customers.

The Other Brand:

Only offers single-channel devices.

Six-Channel Device Features:

Migibio:

Six independent channels allow for simultaneous multi-sample and multi-user operation without waiting. Features include constant temperature functionality, ensuring precise timing, quantification, and temperature control for more stable test results.
