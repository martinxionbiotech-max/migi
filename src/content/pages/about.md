# About Migibio

Guangzhou Magic Biotech Co., Ltd., established in **November 2017** and headquartered in Guangzhou Science City — a national-level innovation park — is a high-tech biotechnology enterprise specializing in the **R&D, production, and sales of POCT veterinary diagnostic equipment and rapid test reagents** for animal disease detection and health assessment.

## Who We Serve

Migibio partners with veterinary professionals and institutions across the diagnostic value chain:

- **Veterinary clinics and animal hospitals** — point-of-care quantitative testing
- **Reference and commercial laboratories** — high-throughput screening
- **Breeding facilities and kennels** — herd health and vaccination monitoring
- **Farms and livestock operations** — on-site disease surveillance
- **Distributors and OEM partners** — white-label diagnostic solutions

## Advanced Manufacturing & Quality System

Our standardized **2,000 m² production workshop** operates under an ISO9001-certified quality management system and GMP principles, with equipment engineered for consistency and scale:

- Automatic laminating machine — precision membrane assembly
- Continuous film stripper — uniform reagent coating
- High-speed strip cutter — accurate lot-to-lot geometry
- Industrial-grade rotary dehumidification system — controlled-environment drying

Every production batch undergoes internal quality control and lot-release testing. Raw materials, intermediates, and finished products are fully traceable through our manufacturing records — a core requirement of our quality commitment.

## Proprietary Technology — FICT

The company's independently developed **Fluorescence Immunochromatographic Technology (FICT)** delivers:

- **Simple operation** — three steps only
- **Rapid** — results as fast as 3 minutes
- **High sensitivity** — limit of detection down to pg/ml
- **Excellent precision** — coefficient of variation (CV%) below 10%
- **High throughput** — up to 200 tests per hour
