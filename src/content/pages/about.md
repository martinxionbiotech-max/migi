# About Migibio

Guangzhou Magic Biotech Co., Ltd., established in **November 2017** and headquartered in Guangzhou Science City — a national-level innovation park — is a high-tech biotechnology enterprise specializing in the **R&D, production, and sales of POCT veterinary diagnostic equipment and rapid test reagents** for animal disease detection and health assessment.

## Company Profile

- **Founded:** November 2017
- **Location:** Guangzhou Science City, Huangpu District, Guangzhou, China
- **Facility:** Standardized 2,000 m² R&D and production workshop
- **Certifications:** ISO9001 and GMP
- **Patents:** 30 core patents
- **Global Reach:** Products sold in 30+ countries and regions (US, UK, Germany, Russia, Peru, India, Vietnam)

## Advanced Manufacturing

The production workshop is equipped with industry-leading instrumentation:

- Automatic laminating machine
- Continuous film stripper
- High-speed strip cutter
- Industrial-grade rotary dehumidification system

## Proprietary Technology — FICT

The company's independently developed **Fluorescence Immunochromatographic Technology (FICT)** delivers:

- **Simple operation** — three steps only
- **Rapid** — results as fast as 3 minutes
- **High sensitivity** — LOD down to pg/ml
- **Excellent precision** — CV% < 10%
- **High throughput** — 200 tests per hour

## Our Team

Our multidisciplinary team spans R&D, quality control, production, and technical support, working together to deliver reliable diagnostics and responsive after-sales service to veterinary professionals worldwide.
