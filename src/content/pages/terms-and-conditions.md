Quotations are prepared for the products, quantities, destination and delivery term stated in them, and remain valid for the period stated in the quotation. Prices are quoted in US dollars unless stated otherwise. Quotations do not constitute an offer capable of acceptance by mere silence: an order becomes binding only when we issue a written order confirmation or proforma invoice.

## 2. Orders and Order Confirmation

- Orders are placed by written purchase order or written confirmation of our proforma invoice, stating the product, catalog number, quantity, destination and requested delivery term.
- We confirm acceptance, price, delivery term, lead time and payment details in the order confirmation, which together with these conditions forms the contract.
- Changes requested after confirmation (quantities, labelling, packaging, shipping destination) may affect price and lead time and are subject to our written agreement.

## 3. Minimum Order Quantities

Most rapid test reagents have a minimum order quantity of **10 boxes**; analyzers have a minimum of **1 unit**. For OEM/ODM and private-label orders, minimums depend on the customization scope, tooling and packaging involved, and are stated in the project quotation.

- Prices are those stated in the order confirmation. Unless stated otherwise, prices do not include import duties, taxes, levies or customs charges in the destination country, which are the buyer's responsibility.
- Payment terms, currency and bank details are stated in the proforma invoice or order confirmation. Where a deposit and balance are agreed, the balance falls due as stated in the order confirmation.
- Bank charges and any intermediary fees are borne by the party instructed to pay them in the order confirmation.

## 5. Delivery, Risk and Title

- Delivery terms are quoted per order — typically **FOB** or **CIF** under **Incoterms 2020**, with the named port stated in the quotation. The agreed Incoterm governs risk, cost and insurance allocation for the shipment.
- Lead time runs from receipt of the confirmed order and, where a deposit is agreed, from receipt of that deposit. Delivery dates are given in good faith and may be affected by production, inspection, freight availability and customs procedures.
- Title passes to the buyer in accordance with the agreed Incoterm and applicable law, on payment in full unless otherwise agreed.

## 6. Inspection and Claims

Please inspect shipments on arrival. Claims for short shipment, incorrect items or visible transit damage should be notified to us promptly and within the period stated in the order confirmation, with photographs, the packing list reference and the lot number, so that we can investigate with the carrier and our quality records. Claims for hidden defects are handled under section 7.

## 7. Warranty

- **Equipment** — analyzers and instruments are warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for the period stated in the order confirmation and the product documentation. Consumable parts and damage caused by misuse, unauthorised modification, incorrect power supply, or failure to follow the IFU are excluded.
- **Reagents and kits** — we warrant that products conform to their agreed specification and to the Instructions for Use at the time of shipment, within their stated shelf life when stored as specified (4–30 °C).
- **Remedy** — where a confirmed non-conformity or defect falls within warranty, we will repair, replace or credit the affected products, at our option, subject to our assessment of the returned goods and lot records.
- The warranty does not cover products that have been stored or handled contrary to the IFU, used beyond their expiry date, or used outside their intended use.

## 8. Returns

Because reagent kits are temperature-sensitive biological products, returns are accepted only where we have agreed in advance and issued return authorisation. In general:

| Situation | Handling |
|---|---|
| Confirmed manufacturing defect or incorrect item supplied | Replacement or credit, with lot traceability records |
| Visible transit damage or short shipment | Notified per section 6, resolved with the carrier and replaced or credited |
| Opened, partially used, expired or incorrectly stored kits | Not returnable, as integrity cannot be verified |
| Change of mind or over-ordering by the buyer | Subject to our written agreement; return freight and any restocking is at the buyer's cost |

Returns must be authorised in writing and shipped in accordance with our instructions, including any documentation required for customs re-entry.
