# News & Industry Insights

## The Shift to Quantitative Point-of-Care Testing

Veterinary diagnostics is undergoing a decisive shift. Traditional qualitative rapid tests — which show a simple line or no line — are being replaced by **quantitative fluorescence immunoassays** that deliver an objective numerical concentration in minutes. This transition is driven by clinical demand for:

- **Earlier diagnosis** — quantitative biomarkers such as SDMA detect kidney disease before clinical signs appear.
- **Objective results** — instrument-read values eliminate subjective line interpretation.
- **Serial monitoring** — numerical trends reveal whether treatment is working, not just whether a pathogen is present.

Fluorescence immunochromatography (FICT) sits at the centre of this transformation. Learn more on our [Core Technology](/core-technology) page.

## How this page is maintained

News items are published when there is a verifiable company or product update: a new analyzer, a certification milestone, an exhibition or a change to the published technical documentation. We do not publish a dated item solely to keep a feed active.

If a dated item is corrected, the page is updated and the correction is visible rather than silently removed. Product claims should be checked against the current IFU and certificate scope; a news item is context, not a substitute for the controlled document.
