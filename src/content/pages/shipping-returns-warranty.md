# Shipping, Returns & Warranty

This page explains how Migibio products are shipped, how to handle them on arrival, and what to do if something is wrong with an order. The delivery term, lead time and claim period applicable to your order are stated in your quotation and order confirmation.

## Shipping Terms

- Delivery terms are quoted per order — typically **FOB** or **CIF** under **Incoterms 2020**, with the named port stated in the quotation. Risk, cost and insurance allocation follow the agreed Incoterm.
- Freight method (air, courier or sea) is selected with the quantity, destination and urgency of the order; analyzers and larger reagent orders are normally shipped by air or sea freight, with courier available for smaller consignments and samples.
- Lead time runs from order confirmation and, where a deposit is agreed, from receipt of that deposit. Delivery dates are given in good faith and depend on production, inspection, freight availability and customs clearance.

## Packaging and Temperature Handling

| Product | Packaging and handling |
|---|---|
| Reagent kits | Sealed foil pouches with desiccant and the ID chip, packed in cartons with the IFU and lot documentation |
| Analyzers | Foam-protected instrument packaging with accessories and manuals, boxed for freight |
| Storage in transit and on arrival | Store at **4–30 °C**; do not freeze |
| Test conditions | Operate at **18–28 °C**; complete testing **within 30 minutes** of opening the pouch |
| Shelf life | As printed on the kit label and stated in the IFU |

Keep the cartons and inner packaging until you have checked the consignment. Where a shipment passes through extreme temperatures, allow the kit to equilibrate to operating temperature before use, as described in the IFU.

## Shipping Documents

Each shipment includes a **commercial invoice** and **packing list**, and the products' **Instructions for Use**. Depending on destination and order, we also provide:

- Certificate of analysis (lot release);
- Safety data sheet (MSDS);
- Certificate of origin;
- Free-sale certificate or other manufacturer documents on request.

Documentation for customs and registration can be requested before shipment, so that any destination-country requirement is settled before the goods leave. See [Regulatory Compliance](/regulatory-compliance/).

## Delivery Issues

**Short shipment, incorrect item or visible transit damage** — report it promptly and in any event within **30 days of receipt**, with photographs, the packing list reference and the lot number. Keep the goods and packaging as received so that the claim can be assessed with the carrier and against our lot records. Confirmed short shipments, wrong items and transit damage are replaced or credited.

**Delayed or held consignments** — send us the tracking or bill of lading reference and we will follow up with the forwarder. Customs holds in the destination country are normally resolved by the importer of record, and we supply the supporting documents you need.

**Temperature excursions** — if a consignment shows signs of temperature exposure, quarantine it and contact us with photographs of the packaging and any indicator strip before using the kits. Do not use kits from a consignment that may have frozen or overheated.

## Returns

Reagent kits are temperature-sensitive biological products, so returns require prior written authorisation:

| Situation | Handling |
|---|---|
| Confirmed manufacturing defect, or an incorrect item supplied | Replacement or credit, traced through the lot number |
| Visible transit damage or short shipment | Notified as above; resolved with the carrier and replaced or credited |
| Opened, partially used, expired or incorrectly stored kits | Not returnable — integrity cannot be verified |
| Ordering error or over-ordering by the buyer | Only with our written agreement; return freight and handling at the buyer's cost |

To request authorisation, contact us with the order reference, catalog number, quantity, lot number and reason. We will confirm whether to return the goods and provide shipping instructions; unauthorised returns cannot be accepted or credited. Where goods are returned across a border, the buyer is responsible for customs documentation and any duties.

## Warranty

- **Analyzers and instruments** — warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for **12 months from delivery**. Wear parts, and damage from misuse, unauthorised modification, incorrect power supply or failure to follow the manual, are excluded.
- **Reagents and kits** — warranted to conform to their agreed specification and to the IFU at the time of shipment, within the printed shelf life when stored as specified (4–30 °C).
- **Claims window** — quality-related claims must be raised within **30 days of receipt**; equipment warranty claims may be made within the 12-month warranty period.
- **What is not covered** — use outside the intended (veterinary) use, use on human samples, storage contrary to the IFU, expiry, or handling that compromises the product's integrity.
- **Making a claim** — contact us with the order reference, product and lot number, purchase date, a description of the problem and, where relevant, QC or result records. We will assess the claim and, where it is confirmed, repair, replace or credit the affected products as described in our [Terms & Conditions of Sale](/terms-and-conditions/).

Every kit carries a lot number, which links it to our production and lot-release records. Please quote it in any support, quality or returns correspondence — it is what allows us to trace the batch, check release data, and resolve your issue quickly.
