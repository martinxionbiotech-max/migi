# Core Technology

Migibio's diagnostic platforms are built on **Fluorescence Immunochromatographic Technology (FICT)**, a quantitative immunoassay technique that combines the specificity of antigen-antibody reactions with the sensitivity of fluorescence detection. This page explains how the technology works, why it outperforms conventional rapid tests, and where it is applied.

## How FICT Works

Fluorescence immunochromatography is a lateral-flow immunoassay in which the detection signal is generated by fluorescent microspheres rather than colloidal gold. The workflow proceeds as follows:

1. **Sample application** — serum, plasma, whole blood, or a swab specimen is applied to the sample well.
2. **Immuno-binding** — fluorescent microspheres labelled with analyte-specific antibodies bind to their target in the specimen.
3. **Capillary migration** — the labelled complex flows along a nitrocellulose membrane and is captured at the test (T) and control (C) lines.
4. **Quantification** — the analyzer excites the captured fluorophores and converts the emitted fluorescence intensity into an objective concentration value.

Because fluorescence intensity is directly proportional to analyte concentration, FICT produces a **quantitative result** — a numerical concentration — rather than a simple positive/negative readout.

## FICT vs. Conventional Rapid Tests

| Dimension | Conventional (Colloidal Gold) | FICT (Fluorescence) |
|---|---|---|
| Result type | Qualitative (line/no line) | Quantitative (concentration) |
| Sensitivity | Lower — limited dynamic range | High — LOD down to pg/ml |
| Precision | Visual, subjective reading | Instrument-read, CV% < 10% |
| Interpretation | Operator-dependent | Objective, reproducible |
| Throughput | Manual, single-read | Automated, up to 200–300 tests/hour |
| Data | No record-keeping | Digital records, cloud curves, reports |

## Applications

FICT underpins a broad range of Migibio diagnostic solutions:

- **Veterinary diagnostics** — infectious disease antigens and antibodies, hormones, inflammatory markers, and kidney biomarkers (e.g., SDMA) for dogs and cats
- **Food safety** — veterinary drug residue and mycotoxin screening
- **Environmental monitoring** — heavy metal ion detection
