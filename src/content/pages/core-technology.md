# Core Technology

Migibio's diagnostic platforms are built on **Fluorescence Immunochromatographic Technology (FICT)**, a quantitative immunoassay technique that combines the specificity of antigen-antibody reactions with the sensitivity of fluorescence detection.

## How FICT Works

1. A sample (serum, plasma, whole blood, swab) is applied to a test device.
2. Fluorescent microspheres labelled with specific antibodies bind to target analytes.
3. The complex migrates along the membrane via capillary action.
4. A fluorescence analyzer quantifies the signal and reports an objective concentration value.

## Key Advantages

| Attribute | FICT Advantage |
|---|---|
| Operation | Three steps only |
| Speed | Results in 3–15 minutes |
| Sensitivity | LOD to pg/ml |
| Precision | CV% < 10% |
| Throughput | Up to 200–300 tests/hour |
| Output | Quantitative + qualitative |

## Applications

- **Veterinary diagnostics** — infectious disease, hormones, inflammatory markers, kidney biomarkers
- **Food safety** — veterinary drug residue, mycotoxin screening
- **Environmental monitoring** — heavy metal ion detection
