# Corporate Identity

## Who We Are

Migibio is the brand of **Guangzhou Magic Biotech Co., Ltd.**, a high-tech biotechnology enterprise specializing in the R&D, production, and sales of POCT veterinary diagnostic equipment and rapid test reagents for animal disease detection and health assessment.

## Our Mission

To make laboratory-grade quantitative diagnostics accessible at the point of care — enabling veterinary professionals worldwide to make faster, more confident clinical decisions.

## Our Values

- **Truth** — scientific rigour and honest communication in everything we build
- **Cooperation** — working as one team with partners, distributors, and clinicians
- **Innovation** — continuous investment in our proprietary fluorescence immunochromatographic technology
- **Excellence** — uncompromising quality control, from raw materials to final release

## Our Commitment to Quality

Every Migibio product is manufactured under ISO9001 and GMP-certified processes. The company also holds a Guangdong Province "Zhuan Jing Te Xin" (Specialized and Innovative) SME designation, a Veterinary Drug Production License, and a 2018 Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition award. We maintain strict lot-release testing, internal quality controls in every test device, and full traceability across our supply chain — because diagnostic accuracy is non-negotiable.

## The Migibio Name

"Migibio" combines "Migi" — drawn from "Magic" (Magic Biotech) — with "bio," reflecting the company's biotechnology foundation. The name signals our dual identity: a scientifically rigorous diagnostics manufacturer and an agile, innovation-driven partner committed to making quantitative veterinary testing accessible worldwide.

## Vision & Spirit

- **Vision:** More sensitive, more efficient!
- **Spirit:** Focus, Innovation, Win-win!

## How to verify the identity behind the brand

Migibio is a brand of Guangzhou Magic Biotech Co., Ltd. For supplier qualification, ask for the business licence, the certificate scope and a recent batch record together, and check that the legal name and address match the quotation and the shipping documents. The brand name alone is not a legal entity.

Where a distributor or marketplace uses the Migibio name, confirm that the seller is authorised for the territory and the product. A certificate or logo without a legal entity, scope and validity date is not enough to establish the manufacturer relationship.
