# Corporate Identity

## Who We Are

Migibio is the brand of **Guangzhou Magic Biotech Co., Ltd.**, a high-tech biotechnology enterprise specializing in the R&D, production, and sales of POCT veterinary diagnostic equipment and rapid test reagents for animal disease detection and health assessment.

## Our Mission

To make laboratory-grade quantitative diagnostics accessible at the point of care — enabling veterinary professionals worldwide to make faster, more confident clinical decisions.

## Our Values

- **Truth** — scientific rigour and honest communication in everything we build
- **Cooperation** — working as one team with partners, distributors, and clinicians
- **Innovation** — continuous investment in our proprietary fluorescence immunochromatographic technology
- **Excellence** — uncompromising quality control, from raw materials to final release

## Our Commitment to Quality

Every Migibio product is manufactured under ISO9001 and GMP-certified processes. We maintain strict lot-release testing, internal quality controls in every test device, and full traceability across our supply chain — because diagnostic accuracy is non-negotiable.

## Vision & Spirit

- **Vision:** More sensitive, more efficient!
- **Spirit:** Focus, Innovation, Win-win!
