## How to use this section as a buyer

1. **Check that each claim carries a method.** A figure without a design behind it cannot be evaluated.
2. **Check the sample type.** A study performed on plasma does not automatically apply to serum.
3. **Check what is missing.** A table with visible `Data pending` rows is more informative than one that looks complete.
4. **Ask for study identifiers.** They are the difference between evidence and assertion.

## The Author's Take

**Position.** This section should be read as an argument rather than a brochure: the claim being made is that a quantitative result is only as good as its documentation.

**Reasoning.** Every competitor can show a number on a screen. What separates platforms in practice is whether the number can be trusted at the low end, compared across visits, and defended to a regulator. Those are documentation properties, not assay properties, which is why this section leads with definitions rather than with specifications. A buyer who finishes these pages should be able to interrogate any vendor, including this one.
