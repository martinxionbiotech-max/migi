# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how **Guangzhou Magic Biotech Co., Ltd.** (广州敏捷生物技术有限公司), the company behind the Migibio brand ("we", "us"), handles personal information collected through **migibio.net** and through our email and WhatsApp business communication.

## Who We Are

Guangzhou Magic Biotech Co., Ltd., G2-601, Najin Technology Industrial Park, No. 39 Ruihe Road, Huangpu District, Guangzhou City, Guangdong Province, China. For any privacy question or request, contact us at martinwang2024@gmail.com.

## Information We Collect

| Category | What it covers | How it reaches us |
|---|---|---|
| Contact and company details | Name, company, country, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, job role | Provided by you in an enquiry, order or support request |
| Enquiry content | Products or catalog numbers of interest, intended market, quantities, technical questions, and any documents you send | Provided by you |
| Transaction data | Order and quotation details, delivery addresses, shipping and customs documents | Created in the course of quoting and supplying an order |
| Technical data | IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, timestamps | Recorded automatically in server and CDN logs when you visit the site |

We do not ask for, and do not want to receive, special-category or sensitive personal data. Please do not send medical, biometric or similar sensitive information about any person.

## Cookies and Tracking

This website does **not** use advertising cookies, social-media pixels or third-party analytics trackers. Essential security cookies may be set by our content delivery network (Cloudflare) to protect the site from bots and abuse; they are used strictly for security and are not used to build a profile of you. Because no non-essential cookies are used, no cookie-consent banner is required on this site. If we ever add analytics or marketing cookies, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it.

## How We Use Your Information

- To answer enquiries and prepare quotations;
- To process, fulfil and ship orders, and to provide technical support;
- To comply with export, customs, tax and accounting obligations;
- To maintain the security and availability of this website;
- To improve our products and the information we publish, based on aggregated enquiry patterns.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails to addresses that contacted us for a quotation, unless you ask us to.

## Legal Bases

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we process personal information on these bases:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Responding to enquiries, quoting, order fulfilment and support | Performance of a contract, or steps taken at your request before a contract |
| Business communication with distributors and partners | Legitimate interests in conducting our business |
| Export, customs, tax and accounting records | Compliance with legal obligations |
| Site security and abuse prevention | Legitimate interests in protecting the site and our visitors |

## Sharing and Disclosure

We share personal information only with parties who need it to provide a service to us, and only for the purposes above:

- hosting, content delivery and IT service providers;
- email and communication service providers;
- freight forwarders, couriers and customs brokers, for orders you place;
- professional advisers such as accountants and auditors;
- authorities or courts, where we are legally required to disclose.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

## International Transfers

We are based in China, and the suppliers listed above may process information in other countries. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we take steps to ensure it remains protected, including contractual measures with our service providers and limiting transfers to what is necessary for the stated purpose.

## Retention

Inquiry and quotation records are kept for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal, accounting and quality-record obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Where a product's quality records are concerned, lot traceability records are retained as required by our quality system and applicable law.

## Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including restricted access, transport encryption on this website, and controlled access to business systems and records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit the data we collect to what we actually need.

## Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
- delete information we no longer need;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive your information in a portable format;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email martinwang2024@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable period and within any deadline set by applicable law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

This website is directed at businesses and veterinary professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal obligations change. The version published on this page, with the "last updated" date below, is the current version. Material changes will be reflected in that date.
