## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which analyzers are compatible with Migibio reagents?**  
A: All Migibio canine and feline fluorescence test reagents are compatible with the FIA680 and FIA880 immunofluorescence analyzers.

**Q: How should test kits be stored?**  
A: Store kits at 4–30℃ until the expiration date. Operate tests at 18–28℃ after opening the device, and complete testing within 30 minutes of opening the pouch.

**Q: What sample types are accepted?**  
A: Most kits accept serum or plasma (EDTA anticoagulant recommended). Refer to the specific IFU for each analyte.
