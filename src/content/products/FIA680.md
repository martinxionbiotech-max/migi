## How FICT Delivers Quantitative Results

The FIA680 is built on Migibio's proprietary Fluorescence Immunochromatographic Technology (FICT). Fluorescent microspheres labelled with analyte-specific antibodies bind to the target biomarker in the specimen and migrate along a nitrocellulose membrane. The analyzer excites the captured fluorophores and converts the emitted fluorescence intensity into an objective concentration (reported in Tu/ml, µg/dL, or ng/ml depending on the analyte).

Because fluorescence intensity is directly proportional to analyte concentration, the FIA680 delivers a true quantitative result — not a subjective line reading. This underpins the analyzer's pg/ml-level sensitivity and CV% below 10% precision, enabling clinicians to track trends and titrate therapy with confidence.

## Three-Step Operation

1. **Add sample** — apply the prepared specimen to the test device.
2. **Insert device** — place the device into the analyzer and select the test item.
3. **Read result** — the analyzer reads the ID chip, counts down, and displays the quantitative result, printing automatically.
