## Constant-Temperature Control: Why It Matters

Fluorescence immunochromatographic reactions are temperature-sensitive: binding kinetics and signal intensity vary with temperature, which can shift measured concentrations. The FIA880's constant-temperature incubation holds the reaction at an optimal temperature, improving lot-to-lot reproducibility and keeping CV% below 10% even under heavy use.
