## FIV is not FeLV

Feline immunodeficiency virus is a lentivirus, while feline leukemia virus is a different retrovirus. FIV testing is usually antibody-based; vaccination can complicate interpretation in some regions, and kittens may carry maternal antibody. A positive result in a healthy cat should be confirmed with an appropriate follow-up test before a lifelong diagnosis is made.

The clinical picture is also different. FIV is associated with immune dysfunction, chronic gingivostomatitis, recurrent infections and weight loss, often after a long asymptomatic period. It is not diagnosed by a FeLV antigen test, and a negative FeLV test does not exclude FIV.
