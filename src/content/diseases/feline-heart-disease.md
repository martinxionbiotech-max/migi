faq:
  - q: "What is NT-proBNP?"
    a: "NT-proBNP (N-terminal pro-B-type natriuretic peptide) is a peptide released when cardiac muscle stretches under pressure or volume overload. Its blood concentration rises with cardiac stress, making it a useful marker for heart disease and congestive heart failure in dogs and cats."
  - q: "Can NT-proBNP tell heart failure from a breathing problem?"
    a: "Yes — this is its most valuable use. In a dyspneic cat or a coughing dog, a high NT-proBNP points toward a cardiac cause, while a low value makes primary respiratory disease more likely. It is a triage tool, not a replacement for echocardiography."
  - q: "Does NT-proBNP replace an echocardiogram?"
    a: "No. NT-proBNP tells you the heart is under stress; an echocardiogram tells you why. They are complementary — NT-proBNP screens and triages, echo characterizes."
